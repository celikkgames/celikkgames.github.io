Pofi – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Pofi is an offline game developed by Rich Man Danışmanlık Limited Şirketi that children can also play. To protect children’s privacy, the game is designed to work with as little data as possible.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, names, email, photos, location, contacts or chat. Your game progress (gold, stars, settings) is stored only on your own device and is never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are configured as “child-directed”: no interest-based (personalized) ads are shown, the advertising ID is not used, and only ads rated for a general audience are requested.

To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as IP address and device type. See the Google Privacy Policy and child-directed settings in AdMob.

In-app purchases and links

There are no real-money purchases in the game. “Gold” and “gems” are in-game points only.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, please contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page.
